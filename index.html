Need to know

Frequently asked questions.

Does Make Payment take me straight to WhatsApp?

No. It reveals the selected offer's payment instructions on this page. WhatsApp opens only when you choose to send proof after making your transfer.

How do I send my payment receipt?

After you transfer, tap “I've Paid — Send Proof on WhatsApp”. Attach the receipt or transaction confirmation in WhatsApp and send the message.

Does sending proof mean I am enrolled immediately?

Not automatically. Your payment must be checked and confirmed by the academy before your enrollment is considered complete.

What if I choose the wrong course?

Return to the course list and tap Make Payment on the correct offer. Confirm the selected course and amount before transferring.
